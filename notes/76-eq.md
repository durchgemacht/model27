# 76 — EQ: a three-band equalizer as a Track FX

The fourth item of the maintainer's list of insert effects (notes/73 §1), built in a session of its own next to Ring
and Reso (notes/73 §16): a three-band equalizer on any track, after Volume + Dist, chosen per sound like every Track
FX. The maintainer preferred a sweepable Mid to a DJ-style split at fixed frequencies (in the main session); the
details were decided on 2026-11-01 (§1). The package `eq` (`tools/machines/eq/`, filter id 5, "EQ"), its generator
`tools/gen_eq.py`, the card's tweak `76-eq.json` (order 76), proof in emulation `tools/emu/test_fltslot.py` (no change
to it: the manifest's `proof` section is EQ's model and cost states). Addresses: VAs of OS 1.13.

## In short

- **Four values on the four RETRIG knobs** (since 0.4, §13: the frequency on SWEEP and the amount on CONTOUR, as the
  other Track FX): SWEEP is **Mid Freq**, the centre of a bell (Q 1) from 200 Hz through 1 kHz to about 4.9 kHz;
  CONTOUR is **Mid Gain**; COLOR is **Low Gain**, a low shelf at 100 Hz; SHAPE is **High Gain**, a high shelf at 8 kHz.
  Every gain is −12 dB at 0, flat at the centre (64), +11.8 dB at 127. The first Track FX with four values:
  descriptors 3 and 4 (COLOR, SHAPE) are active in a build for the first time.
- **Flat is dry**: with Low, High and Mid at 64 the track passes untouched, bit for bit, and a sound that steps to EQ
  starts there (High's start word is the centre, CONTOUR's neutral word being 0).
- **The bands** are the Audio EQ Cookbook's biquads (R. Bristow-Johnson): shelves with the slope S = 1, a peaking bell
  of constant Q (a cut is the mirror of the same boost). They run in direct form I on the EMAC; their coefficients come
  from tables written by the generator, worked out only when a value moves. A boost of a hot track clips at full
  scale; it never wraps.
- **Cost**, counted by the proof (EQ 0.3, §12): 843 instructions a track and block with all three bands on, 895 with
  High at +12 dB, 1 058 with every value jumping every block, 1 109 in the block a track comes to it, 57 flat, 183
  silent; six tracks at most 6 654 a block, 6.2 % (0.1 and 0.2: 7 188, 6.6 %; Crush 7.3 %, Samples 8.3 %); with
  0.4's words one instruction less each (§13). Not measured on the machine yet.
- **Checked on the host**: the device object in the interpreter is its host build bit for bit (3 000 blocks), the host
  build is clean under the undefined-behaviour sanitizer over the words' corners, and its response is within 0.02 dB
  of the Cookbook's (§6). The proof, `test_fltslot.py`, in the maintainer's run: **ALL OK** but the cost line, by
  design (§7), EQ alone and in a table of eight; its four values through the knob lookup, the LFO, the p-locks and the
  record.

## 1. Decisions (2026-11-01)

From the main session, before this one: Low on SWEEP, High on CONTOUR, the Mid's gain on COLOR and its frequency on
SHAPE; ±12 dB, flat at the centre; a bell from about 200 Hz to 5 kHz, centred on about 1 kHz; flat must be untouched
and must be where a sound starts; boosts saturate. Asked here, one at a time, each with a recommendation:

| Question | Decided | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Where do the shelves sit? (100 Hz / 8 kHz, 80 Hz / 12 kHz, 150 Hz / 6 kHz) | 100 Hz and 8 kHz, the recommendation | just outside the Mid's range, so the bands overlap little; High's coefficients fit over 4 (b0 reaches 2.47 at +12 dB; at 4 kHz it would be 4.6, over 8) |
| How wide is the Mid? (Q 0.7, 1, 1.4) | Q 1, about 1.4 octaves, the recommendation | the bell's coefficients stay under 2 at every centre and gain |
| How do the values read? | Low, High, Mid −64 .. 63 (Samples' Cutoff's format), Mid Freq 0 .. 127, the recommendation | dB or Hz on screen would need a formatter of the package's own, which `test_fltslot.py` cannot check yet (it knows `int`, `centre` and `cutoff`): later, with a change to the proof |

## 2. The values

Since 0.4 (§13); in 0.1 to 0.3 k 28 was Low, k 29 High, k 30 Mid, k 31 Mid Freq.

| Where | What | Word | Neutral / start |
|---|---|---|---|
| k 28 (RETRIG + SWEEP, CC 74) | Mid Freq: the bell's centre, 1000 Hz · 5^((k − 64) / 64) | 0..0x7f00: 200 Hz at 0, 1 kHz at 0x4000, 4 874 Hz at 0x7f00 | 0x4000 (the slot's) |
| k 29 (RETRIG + CONTOUR, CC 71) | Mid: the bell's gain, 12 dB · (k − 64) / 64 | 0..0x7f00, 0x4000 flat | 0 (the slot's); EQ's start word 0x4000 |
| k 30 (RETRIG + COLOR) | Low: the low shelf's gain, the same | 0..0x7f00, 0x4000 flat | 0x4000 |
| k 31 (RETRIG + SHAPE) | High: the high shelf's gain, the same | 0..0x7f00, 0x4000 flat | 0x4000 |

k is the word / 256 (fractions between knob steps, as the OS's smoothing and the LFO give them). Formats: Low, High
and Mid `cutoff` (−64 .. 0 .. 63), Mid Freq `int` (0 .. 127). Names (since 0.2, §11; in this order since 0.4): EQ Mid
Freq / MIDF, EQ Mid Gain / MIDG, EQ Low Gain / LOWG, EQ High Gain / HIGG (the short ones are what the LFO's
destination shows). Words outside 0..0x7f00 (an LFO can push
them there) are clamped. A step of 0.1875 dB per knob step; the top step is +11.8 dB, so the knob reads 63 there.

## 3. The design

### 3.1 The bands

Each band is the Cookbook's biquad, normalized by a0: `y = b0 x + b1 x1 + b2 x2 − a1 y1 − a2 y2`. Low's poles and
zeros depend on its gain only (its corner is fixed), High's likewise, the Mid's on its gain and its centre. The order
is Low, then Mid, then High.

### 3.2 On the EMAC

The slot calls a Track FX with MACSR 0xa0 (fractional, saturating) and the accumulators clear (fltslot.c). The
coefficients do not fit Q31 as they are (|a1| approaches 2 at every low corner, High's b0 reaches 2.47), so they are
stored over 2^shift and the sum, which is `y / 2^shift`, is read and doubled shift times:

| Band | Largest coefficient (±12 dB) | Stored | Read, then |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low (100 Hz) | 1.99 (b1, a1) | over 2 | one doubling |
| Mid (200 Hz .. 5 kHz, Q 1) | 1.99 (a1 at 200 Hz) | over 2 | one doubling |
| High (8 kHz) | 2.47 (b0 at +12 dB) | over 4 (since 0.3 over 2 below +9.2 dB, §12) | two doublings (one) |

The EMAC's read saturates at ±1.0, which here is ±2 or ±4 full scale. Each doubling is an `add.l` that branches on
overflow to full scale with the sign the value had: a band's output clips at full scale and never wraps, and the
clipped value is what the band feeds back (direct form I keeps its outputs as states). The scale factor of the MAC
instructions (`<< 1`) cannot do it: the EMAC ignores it in fractional mode (notes/69 §16).

- **One band** (`eq_frames`): its five coefficients in a2-a6, its four states in d1-d4, two frames a turn with the
  states' roles turned round, so that nothing is moved: `x_n` is loaded into the register of `x2` once `b2 x2` is in
  the accumulator, `y_n` read into the one of `y2`. 11 instructions a frame over 2, 13 over 4.
- **All three** (`eq_frames3`): one loop runs the bands frame by frame, each in an accumulator of its own (acc0 Low,
  acc1 Mid, acc2 High), the eight states of the chain in d0-d7 (the input's two frames and each band's output's two,
  each the next band's input). The fifteen coefficients come from memory with the products: each `mac`/`msac` loads
  the next one (a MAC with load, a form the OS uses itself), into a3 and a4 in turn; the pointer goes back before the
  frame's last product, which loads the next frame's first. A band's `b2 x2` goes into its accumulator before the band
  ahead writes its newest frame over that register. 30 instructions a frame instead of 35, and no load and store of
  the block between the bands.
- **The host model** (`eq.c`, `#ifndef __mcoldfire__`): the same arithmetic in C, each product floored to 2^-39, the
  sum read floored to Q31 and saturated, then the saturated doublings; `eq_frames3` runs the three bands one after the
  other, which is the same arithmetic in each band's accumulator. The proof compiles it as EQ's model.

### 3.3 The coefficients

Tables written by `tools/gen_eq.py` (`eq_tab.h`, 10 860 bytes), from the Cookbook's formulas in floating point,
rounded to Q31 over 2^shift:

- `EQ_LOW`, `EQ_HIGH`: b0, b1, a1, a2 at every knob step of gain (129 rows, k = 0 .. 128); the word's low byte between
  two rows (Samples' `lerp`: 8 bits of the difference dropped).
- `EQ_MID`: b0, a1, a2 on a grid of gain every 8 knob steps (17 rows) by centre every 4 (33 columns), bilinear.
  Measured before choosing (exact interpolation of the Cookbook's coefficients, every 64th word of both, against the
  exact bell, 20 Hz to 20 kHz): every 4 × 4 0.044 dB at most (13 KB), every 8 × 4 0.044 dB (6.7 KB), every 4 × 2
  0.011 dB (26 KB), every 8 × 8 0.17 dB, every 16 × 4 0.056 dB. The gain hardly matters, the centre does.
- **The last coefficient from the others**, so that a band is flat where it should be whatever the rounding: Low at
  the top of the spectrum (`b2 = 1 + (b1 − a1) + (a2 − b0)`, from b0 − b1 + b2 = 1 − a1 + a2), High at DC
  (`b2 = 1 + (a1 − b1) + (a2 − b0)`), the Mid at both (`b1 = a1`, `b2 = 1 + (a2 − b0)`). Summed from the small
  differences: `1 − a1 + …` leaves 32 bits on the way (§4).
- **Only when a value moves**: each track keeps the words its coefficients were worked out for; a band whose gain is
  flat is skipped and its coefficients wait. A reset forgets the words (−1), so the next block works them out.
- `gen_eq.py --response` reads the tables as `eq.c` does and compares with the Cookbook: Low at most 0.021 dB, High
  0.0004 dB, the Mid 0.043 dB; at the ends Low 0 / 127 give −11.97 / +11.79 dB at 20 Hz and 0.0000 dB at 20 kHz, High
  −11.99 / +11.80 dB at 20 kHz and 0.0000 at 20 Hz, the Mid −12.00 / +11.81 dB at its centre at 200 Hz, 1 kHz and
  4.9 kHz.

### 3.4 The block

1. A track out of range: nothing.
2. **Flat** (Low, High and Mid at exactly 0x4000): the block is not touched, bit for bit, and each band's states
   follow the input (x1 = y1 = the last frame, x2 = y2 = the one before), so that a gain turned away from the centre
   starts from where the signal is.
3. After a reset (the track coming to EQ), a block that is not flat starts the states at its first frame.
4. **Silence**: if Low's newest input and High's newest output are under 2^14 (−102 dB, Samples' level), then every
   state, and the block is all 0, the block stays silent and the states are cleared.
5. The words clamped; the coefficients of the bands that are on, where their words moved.
6. All three on: `eq_frames3`. Otherwise each band that is on runs over the block (`eq_frames`), and a band that is
   flat passes its input on and follows it, as in 2.

The trig bit is not read: an equalizer has no notes. Mono, 32 frames at 48 kHz, on the track's block in place.

### 3.5 Space

13 372 bytes of code and tables (the tables 10 860, `eq_frames` and `eq_frames3` 848, the C about 1 660), 708 bytes of
variables (six tracks of 118: twelve states, fifteen coefficients, four words, a flag). With the filter slot and the
shortcut layer the tweak adds 19 044 bytes to the image.

## 9. Open points

- `[HYP]` The device's EMAC as `tools/emu/emac.py` models it, as for Samples and POTS: a sum's partial values may pass
  ±1.0 within the accumulator (its guard bits), only the read saturates; the MAC with load reads its operands before it
  writes the loaded register. The OS uses both. The machine will tell.
- Truncation's bias: each read floors (on average half an LSB of `y / 2^shift` low), and a band's feedback carries it
  to DC with the gain 1 / (1 + a1 + a2), largest for Low's boost, whose poles sit near DC. Measured on the host build
  (a 1 kHz sine, the output's mean): Low +12 dB −11 600 LSB (−105 dBFS), Low −12 dB −2 900 (−117 dBFS), the Mid +12 dB
  at 200 Hz −1 500 (−123 dBFS), every band at +12 dB with the Mid at 200 Hz −13 100 (−104 dBFS), the most. Inaudible
  DC, and under 2^14 (16 384), so the states still count as rung out and silence after a sound is cleared to 0 (§6).
  Rounding instead (half an LSB into each accumulator: one more product a band and frame, about 96 instructions a
  block) was left out; MACSR's rounding mode was not used, as `emac.py`'s model of it is not checked against the
  device.
- dB and Hz on screen: a formatter of the package's own, once `test_fltslot.py` can check one (§1).

## 13. The frequency on SWEEP: EQ 0.4 (2026-11-01)

The maintainer, in the main session: « beide tauschen. mach auch den wechsel beim eq, sweep=midf ». Every other
Track FX has its frequency on SWEEP and its amount on CONTOUR (Samples Cutoff / Reso, Crush Rate / Bits, Ring Freq /
Mix, Reso Pitch / Amount), as the stock machines have a pitch or a movement on SWEEP and a depth on CONTOUR. The main
session had recommended keeping the shelves on SWEEP and CONTOUR for their CCs; the maintainer chose Mid Freq on
SWEEP knowingly: Low and High lose CC 74 and 71, Mid Freq and the Mid's gain get them. Low on COLOR and High on SHAPE,
so that the pair runs from low to high.

| RETRIG + SWEEP (k 28, CC 74) | EQ Mid Freq / MIDF | EQ Low Gain / LOWG |

| RETRIG + COLOR (k 30) | EQ Low Gain / LOWG | EQ Mid Gain / MIDG |
