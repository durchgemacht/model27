Organize your +Drive

MIDI ports:

To upload

    Drop files and folders here, or onto a folder in the list. Drag rows onto another folder to move them, onto a folder on the right to upload them there. Enter or a double-click renames. Each file becomes a 48 kHz mono sample; what is not audio is left out.

    On your Cycles

      Click a folder to open it, a sample to select it. Select puts a box on each row: then a click picks folders too, Shift a run. Deleting cannot be undone. The Cycles stays silent while it writes.

      Warning

      Your audio files go from this browser to your Cycles, nowhere else. Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer (Web MIDI). From Cycles One-Shot, MIT license.
