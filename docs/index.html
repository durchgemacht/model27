The Model:Cycles, plus a little.

A small, curated firmware for the Model:Cycles: four-knob machines and a short list of mods that work together, flashed from your browser in about 30 seconds.

Open the flasher Read the guide

The mods

  1. SAMPLE One sample per trig from the +Drive, picked in the PRESET browser and saved with the sound, with the Model:Samples' filter and a waveform view. Cycles One-Shot, by durchgemacht
  2. Samples OS The Model:Cycles starts as a Model:Samples and plays your own samples. From Modded-Cycles

model27 is a downstream fork of Modded-Cycles, started from its release 1.55, the last one with a mod picker. It keeps the picker and offers a curated set of cards: the maintainer's own mods first, then mods by other authors, each reviewed against the Cycles Mod Standard so that mods from different people fit together. The other Modded-Cycles mods stay buildable from the command line. Every mod and tool keeps its author's name: on the flasher's cards and credits, in the guide and in the repository's third-party notices.
