# AGENTS.md — docs/

The GitHub Pages site, served from `main`, folder `docs/`: <https://durchgemacht.github.io/model27/>. Static files, no build step,
no framework. Read the root [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) first.

```

                       the user's MAIN OS
```

## What the flasher shows

The main page (redesign of 2026-10-31, 1-bit look in `flasher/style.css`, graded on 15/11/2026: see *The look*) offers
the fast method only, on two screens: the cards (with *Back to the official firmware* below them), then the install
steps. Step 1, *Your machine* (17/11/2026, notes/57 §7), asks which device you own and, for a Model:Samples, whether it
already runs mods from this page; one sentence on the right says what will happen, the rest is folded under *Good to
know*. Steps 2 to 4 keep the numbers the texts refer to. In step 2 a file the page took lights a green LED in its drop zone
(`--led`, as the manager's connected); its status lines show only errors, warnings and the build under way, the rest
stays for screen readers (`setFileStatus()`). Step 4 lists what is installed (`#picked`) and holds the one box to tick;
its old summary line stays in the page, hidden, for the smoke test. A Model:Samples that refuses its way back (a Cycles
OS checking with the Cycles key: by hand, or by a tool without the 32-byte change) gets, once and by itself, the
Samples OS in the Model:Cycles container (`useSbackAlt()`, notes/57 §7).
The Official firmware and Samples OS tabs, the old machine picker (`flasher/?samples` is gone) and, with `CLASSIC_UI`
(false), the classic method and Advanced stay in the page, hidden, and the smoke test still drives them. The cards sit in four sections by kind (`kindOf()`: added
machines, effects, Track FX, mods); only the exceptions carry a tag (Experimental), a card's Details say how it was
tested. Each section but Mods has a How to use button next to its title (no line under it: the window says it all)
that opens one window over the page (`openInfo()`): `kind_<k>_info`, blocks of `[heading, text]` with what all the
section's cards share, and `kind_<k>_note`, an optional smaller note at the end. A card does not repeat its window.
A card's Details button opens its description and facts in the same window (`openCard()`), so the cards never move;
the card keeps a hidden copy (`#det-<id>`), which the search and the smoke test read.

The footer (the maintainer's design of 14/11/2026): the brand, the site's and the project's links, and in a third of
the width a receipt (`#rc` in `flasher/index.html`, `wireReceipt()` in `app.js`): "Do not buy me a coffee", the people
who did the hard part as its items, model27's cut 0.00. model27 asks for no money; each name links to that person's
GitHub profile (`https://github.com/<name>`) and nowhere else: no Ko-fi, donation or payment pages (the maintainer's
choice of 15/11/2026; Émilie Gillet is `pichenettes`). One exception, the maintainer's of 16/11/2026: Adam Szabo, who
has no GitHub work behind model27, links to his thesis on his own site (MAXSAW's model). Below it: the credits (folded, with the licenses), the
disclaimer and the version. Light only, as the
page; the receipt's torn edges are the tokens `--zig-top` and `--zig-bot`, with a dark pair.

## The look

The flasher, the uploader, the Wavetables page and the manager share one look (the maintainer's choices of 15/11/2026,
release 0.67, tried first on a copy of the site with a switch per change; the manager followed on 16/11/2026): ink on a
cool grey, light only.

- **Lines** say what they are: 2 px a frame (a card, a pane, a window, a step), 1.5 px a control (a button, a field,
  a checkbox, a tab), 1 px a divider inside a frame (a card's foot, a step's head, dashed section lines) and the rules
  between a list's rows, which are grey (`--rule`).
- **Two text tones**: the ink (`--ink`, a dark blue-grey, `#17222e`) for names, titles and what you act on; `--soft`
  for what explains (a card's sentence, hints, sizes, credits, footers), `--soft-inv` for the same on an inverted row.
- **Controls** have 3 px corners (`--r`) and normal writing; headings, the nav and the receipt keep their capitals.
  A filled button is a block of ink, a step lighter under the pointer (`--ink-up`); an outlined one gets a faint
  ground (`--tint`); nothing moves. Fields and checkboxes sit on `--field`, lighter than the card. Disabled is a
  dashed frame. The uploader's `.tool` is the small outlined button; the Wavetables page uses the same for what
  were underlined words (`button.tool` in the editor).
- **A card's shadow** is a 1 px checkerboard in `--sh` (the light soft tone): a block behind the card (`.mod::after`,
  on the Wavetables page the status bar's box), 4 px down and right, cut by the mask `--chk`, since a box-shadow
  takes no pattern. A window over the page has the same checkerboard, 8 px, in the ink (`--sh-win`): the light one
  is lost on the dithered backdrop. For that the `<dialog>` only places the window (no frame, no ground) and its
  box is `.info-in` / `.alert-in`, which has the frame and scrolls: a pseudo-element of a box that scrolls is
  clipped. The Wavetables page's help and Save windows have it too (4 and 8 px). The footer's receipt keeps a full
  shadow in the ink (a drop-shadow that follows its torn edges).
- **A window over the page** (the flasher's How to use and Details, the uploader's warning): while it is open the page
  behind it does not scroll (`app.js` sets `html.modal`, the sheet hides the overflow and keeps the scrollbar's
  gutter). The flasher's window opens with its title focused, not Close, so no focus ring stands round the button;
  Close is a `.tool`.
- **Keys and knobs in a text** (`rich()`): a key is an inverted box with the controls' corners and letters lighter
  than the page (`--field`); a knob has the same size and weight and grey dashes under it (`--rule`).
- **One typeface**, IBM Plex Mono (Google Fonts, weights 400 to 700; 600 for controls, 500 for small print).
- States are still borders, dashes and inversion, never colour. The spacing between sections and in the page's head
  is as before (the maintainer's wish: thinner lines need the room).

- **The one colour**: the manager's green LED for "connected" (`--led`, the maintainer's choice of 16/11/2026), in
  its chip, its setup's first step and on the tile of the firmware on the Cycles; only there for now.
- **No button that looks locked for what only needs the Cycles**: such a button stays a button and connects first
  (the manager's `withCycles()`); a dashed frame is left for what cannot work (no Web MIDI, something running).

The tokens live in each page's own sheet (`flasher/style.css`, `uploader/style.css`, `manager/style.css`, the
editor's `<style>`): keep them alike. Plain CSS only (no nesting, `color-mix` or `:has`). The home page and the guide
are not part of it: they keep their own.

## The manager

`manager/` (release 0.30, notes/77) keeps one project per firmware on the Model:Cycles: a firmware is a `.syx` the user
adds (any build, the official OS named by its hash in `KNOWN`), with its backups and its slot on the Cycles. Switch
runs five steps in a dialog: stop the sequencer, back up (request `0x6F`), flash (`upgradeFast` of `flasher.js`, the
user presses YES), bring the firmware's project back (checked by the settings the Cycles answers to `0x64`), save it in
its slot. It is not tied to the flasher: it only loads `flasher.js` and links nothing else. The library lives in the
browser's IndexedDB under the page's origin (an export from another address imports as is). `style.css` has *The
look* above since 16/11/2026, its own copy of the tokens, header, buttons and footer. The overview is the firmwares'
tiles: the one on the Cycles has the lightest ground, the LED (Active), Mark another as active and Back up now; the
Cycles' card above them is a compact row shown only while the page needs something (no firmware yet, which one is on
the Cycles). A firmware named as the flashers name their builds (`model-cycles_OS1.13_<mod>+…`) shows as
"Model:Cycles build" with its mods counted and listed behind Details; one from this flasher, which names its builds
`model-cycles_OS1.13_flasher=model27_v=<release>_mods=<mod>+….syx` since 16/11/2026 (key=value parts, so that the
manager can read what made it), as "model27 build" with the release; any other long name cut to a line (`looks()`,
`shortName()`, `nameParts()` in `app.js`). The setup's four steps and the switch's texts speak to the owner (*Writing
for users*); the setup no longer asks for the slot, the switch's last step does. Class names that `site.css` styles too
(`.wrap`, `.top`, `.brand`, `.btn`, `.foot`) are overridden there, and the page's own classes avoid the others.
Light only, as the flasher. Its link in the site's nav comes after the Uploader on every page, and in the
flasher's footer (shown again on 16/11/2026, the maintainer's choice; hidden from 09/11/2026 until then). Checks: `tools/webmanager_check.sh` (the scripts on the host); the page itself against
the stand-in `tools/manager/fake-cycles.js` (serve the repository's root, load it by hand, `FakeCycles.install()`),
and on a Model:Cycles by the maintainer.

## The uploader

`uploader/` (release 0.49) puts audio files on the Cycles' +Drive: the list to upload on the left, a tree of folders
(`state.tree`: files and folders from the chooser, Add folder's `webkitRelativePath` or a drop's entries, audio only;
New folder, drag to move, Enter or a double-click to rename), the Cycles' folder on the right (its path as links,
`+Drive › …`; a click on a folder's row opens it, the second click of a double-click is dropped). Rows are selected as
in a file manager (click, ⌘/Ctrl, Shift, ⌘A, Escape; on the right a folder only with ⌘/Ctrl or Shift, or with Select
on, which shows a box on each row and makes a click pick instead of open); the selection is deleted on the Cycles, a folder with everything in it, after one `window.confirm` (as the manager asks), or taken off the list.
Upload sends the whole list into the folder shown; rows dragged onto a folder on the right, or files dropped there from
the computer, go into that folder. Folders are made on the Cycles before what is in them (`jobs()`); a name already in
a folder is skipped, not replaced. Each file is converted in the browser to a 48 kHz mono sample and written with the
OS's own file commands (Transfer protocol, `0x14`-`0x16`, `0x24`,
`0x43`-`0x45`; how they were found: Cycles One-Shot's `TECHNICAL.md`). Chromium (Chrome, Edge and Opera, the browsers
the page names) decodes no AIFF and no WAV of 64-bit floats, so `elektron.js` reads those itself and hands the browser
the same samples as a WAV (`audioInfo`, `toWav`; release 0.57, compared byte for byte with afconvert's WAVs and in
Chromium 152); `audioInfo` also gives each way a file fails its own message in `convert()` (compressed and how, empty,
too long by its header, damaged, unreadable), and `tools/uploader/audiofiles.js` builds the tests' WAV and AIFF files.
A sample the Cycles refuses to delete no longer stops the rest (release 0.58): `deleteSelected()` collects every one,
the folders with one in them stay, and the window names them with `lockedHelp()`'s blocks (Why, then the steps To
delete it): once a track uses a sample the Cycles locks it, and a restart unlocks it only if no track uses it anymore
(tried on a Model:Cycles with one SAMPLE track, 10/11/2026). The window's blocks (`help` of `show()`) are a heading and
a sentence or steps, styled as the flasher's How to use (`.info-body h3`, `ol.howto`); more than three files
(`ALERT_ROWS`) fold into a `<details>` that scrolls, as the flasher's "Something went wrong?". `fake-cycles.js` takes
`locked: [paths]` to try it in a browser.
In `/Wavetables` it applies CHORD Tables'
rules (notes/66). The status line under the title says what happened last; a warning or a problem also opens a window
over the page (`alertBox()`, a `<dialog>` in `index.html`), which stays until OK or Escape and names the files it is
about, a second message going under the first (release 0.53, the maintainer's wish of 08/11/2026). It came from Cycles One-Shot's upload page (MIT, same author), whose old address now leads here;
this copy is the one kept up. `style.css` came from the manager's and now follows the flasher's (*The look*): the
same tokens, header, buttons and footer, overridden on `site.css`'s classes as there; over each list a line names
its columns (`#files-cols`, `#folder-cols`, hidden by `app.js` while the list has no rows). Light only. It is not tied to the other pages
and loads only `release.js` and `site.js`. Checks: `tools/webuploader_check.sh` (the protocol, and the page against a
stand-in, on the host); the page itself against `tools/uploader/fake-cycles.js` (serve the repository's root, open
`/docs/uploader/`, load it by hand, `FakeDrive.install()`), and on a Model:Cycles by the maintainer.

## The Wavetables page

`wavetables/index.html` (release 0.55) is the editor of CHORD Wave Bank, the maintainer's own repository (MIT, by
durchgemacht and Claude): one HTML file, `editor/chordwave-editor.html` there, built from its `editor/template.html`
with the Braids and Plaits wave tables in it. It fills CHORD's 31 frames, plays them, and writes them into the MAIN OS
of the file the user drops: the official OS 1.13, or a build whose bootloader parts, CHORD's pointer table and the
part of MAIN OS copied into SRAM are those of OS 1.13, as in this flasher's Model:Cycles builds (checked on
CHORD Tables alone and on all fifteen cards, 2026-11-09); it refuses the others, Model:Samples containers among them.
Since release 0.56 it also saves the table for CHORD Tables (Save .wav): the OS's sine and the 31 frames, 48 kHz,
mono, 16 bits, named `NN <name>.wav` by the number chosen beside the button, which the Uploader writes unchanged.
Everything runs in the browser. `tools/gen_wavetables_page.py --editor <its chordwave-editor.html>` writes the page:
the editor as it is, with this site's title and icon, its header and nav (classes `.site-top`, `.site-brand`: the
editor has a `.brand` of its own; no `site.css`), a footer with model27's version, and `release.js` and `site.js`;
it hides the editor's Examples and Fill slots from a pattern (`#ex-card`, `#pat-row`, the maintainer's choice of
09/11/2026; they stay in the DOM).
Change the editor in its repository, rebuild it there, and run the generator again; `--check` says whether the page
is still the editor's (the `?v=` stamp and the version in the footer aside, so a stamp bump by hand passes) and
whether the six pages have the same nav. Light only. CHORD Tables reads CHORD's own table at run time, so with it, 0
plays the page's wavetable. Checks: the editor's own (in its repository), `--check` here, and on a Model:Cycles by
the maintainer.

The whole site is in English (the maintainer's decision of 31/10/2026): no French, no `data-l` spans, no EN/FR

## Writing for users

Every text a user reads (cards, section windows, guide, home page, release notes) follows these rules, drawn from the
Model:Cycles and Syntakt manuals, Korg's monologue manual and teenage engineering's EP-133 guide (the maintainer's
decision of 04/11/2026). Existing texts are brought to them group by group: the Track FX cards, the effects, the
mods, the guide, the rest.

- **The reader** owns a Model:Cycles and knows its manual, not our code. They want to know what to press and what
  they will hear.
- **The manual's words**: preset (not sound), parameter lock, preset lock, LFO destination, Control All, MACHINES
  menu, PRESET menu, GRID RECORDING mode, signal path, recording. Never words from the code or the notes: record,
  slot, k 32, descriptor, id, neutral, real parameter, dearest, block, claim, take.
- **Where a thing lives, as the player sees it, not as the code stores it.** A Track FX is on a track (an insert
  effect), an effect on a pattern, a machine on a track. "preset" only where something is saved or loaded: the
  code keeps the Track FX in the sound record, but "each preset can have one Track FX" is wrong for the reader.
- **Keys and knobs.** A key in brackets in the source: `[RETRIG]`, `[TRIG] key`, `[FUNC] + [MACHINES]`; the page shows
  it as an inverted box, without the brackets. Keys to choose from share a box, with slashes: "press
  `[TRIG 8/9/11/12]`", then what each does in that order. A knob or parameter in capitals: PITCH, SWEEP, LEVEL/DATA,
  DELAY TIME, VOLUME + DIST; the page underlines the names in `KNOBS` with dashes. Both by `rich()` in
  `flasher/app.js` (classes `key` and `knob`). Settings and machine names in capitals, unmarked: OFF, CHORD.
- **One action per sentence.** It starts with the verb and names every key, the held keys first, in the order the
  hands move: "In GRID RECORDING mode, press and hold a [TRIG] key and [RETRIG], then turn the knob." Never leave a
  key for the reader to fill in ("…, LFO to make them …").
- **Then what you hear or see**, in a sentence of its own. No filler ("as usual", "they work like the other
  knobs", "real parameters"). A range at the end, in brackets, only where it is not the usual 0–127: (-64–63).
- **Only what you need to start** on a card or in a window. Background, measurements and history go to the guide
  or the notes.
- **Structure.** A card: `short`, one sentence on what it does to the sound, in musicians' words (not "on any track":
  its section's window says where it works); the Details, what the short does not say, then the controls, never the
  short again. A section window and, as they are rewritten, the Details: blocks, each a short heading (the action or
  the parameter) and one or two sentences. In a card's Details, a block per knob: the heading the knob alone
  (SWEEP), then a whole sentence on what it sets, the term written out and the screen's abbreviation in brackets
  ("Sets the sample rate (RATE)."). A card whose mod has a page of its own (the CPU page, RESAMPLE): a block per
  setting of that page, its heading the name the page shows, not the knob. A default goes into that sentence ("127, the default, is off"), not a block of
  its own. The name the screen shows starts the card's short text, without brackets: "CRU - Makes a track grainy …"
  (`screen` in `FEAT`, none where it is the card's name). `Note:` at the end, smaller, only for what protects the user's work. What all
  the cards of a section share lives in its window, not on each card.
- **Before showing a text**: every key combination checked against the code or the maintainer's test, every word as
  above, and each sentence one a Model:Cycles owner can follow without guessing a word.

## Adding a mod to the flasher

1. Add it to `FEATURES` in `tools/gen_flasher_tweaks.py` (`"status": "experimental"`, `cat`, `credit`, `license` when
   the mod carries outside code, `excludes`, `includes`, `needs` or `requires` if any), then
   `python3 tools/gen_flasher_tweaks.py` to regenerate `flasher/tweaks.js`. `cat` orders the card inside its
   section (machines, effects, mods: `kindOf()` in `app.js`), one of `CATS` (`pack`, `sound`, `seq`, `live`, `screen`,
   `io`); without it the card comes last in its section. The order of `FEATURES` is the build order (the `REF_MAINOS`
   keys); the page shows the cards by section, then `CATS`, then `FEATURES` order.
   `"requires": "<card id>"` is for an add-on that only works with another card (sample preview needs Model-TG):
   ticking it ticks that card too (with that card's own exclusions), unticking that card unticks it, its Details say
   "Works with …", and `ref_mainos.py` only lists combinations that hold both. Give the add-on the same `with` as the
   card it requires (sample preview takes `33-sample-preview-st` when Model-TG takes `30-model-tg-st`), and make the
   `requires` of each of its tweak files point at the matching tweak (`sample-preview` → `model-tg`,
   `sample-preview-st` → `model-tg-st`); `gen_flasher_tweaks.py` refuses a card that doesn't.
2. `python3 tools/check_overlaps.py`, then `python3 tools/ref_mainos.py --cycles … --syntakt …`: it rewrites `REF_MODS`
   (the hash of each tweak's writes and payload, checked by the page on every build) and `REF_MAINOS` (the whole MAIN
   OS of a sample: each mod alone, every pair, the largest combinations) in `flasher/app.js` (notes/49). A build whose
   mod or listed MAIN OS differs is refused; a combination outside the sample is built and checked mod by mod. The
   rules between mods (`excludes`, `includes`, a card's `requires`, `with`, the Syntakt engines' `tg` version) are read
   by `ref_mainos.py` as `app.js` reads them: a new kind of rule goes into both (`tools/webflash_smoke.js` ticks each card alone and every
   pair in the page and checks that each one is in the sample).
3. In `flasher/app.js`: the card texts in `FEAT`, as in *Writing for users*: `label`, the name alone (no "(effect)", no half sentence);
   `short`, one plain sentence on the card (about 80 characters, what it does on the machine); `desc`, the Details,
   a few short paragraphs separated by a blank line (`\n\n`): what it is, the buttons, the main limit, the rest in
   the guide; optional `note` (shown once ticked), only for what protects the user, one or two short sentences; `clash` if it
   has `excludes` (why it doesn't go with the other mod), `parts` for a pack (what it brings), a `short` per engine
   (`"eng-<code>"`) for an engine card. And the guide anchor in `GUIDE_OF` (one line per mod).
4. In `tools/webflash_smoke.js`: the expected order of tweaks, the expected **display** order of the rows (by
   section), and a check that its card and its guide link render.
5. A guide section in `guide/index.html` (next number, before *Samples OS*; renumber the ones after): a lede, the
   steps (`<ol>`) with the buttons in `<b>`, then `<dl class="facts">` with the limits and a *Tested* / *Experimental*
   line saying how it was checked. Copy the `#tempo` or `#trig-hold` section.
6. A new entry at the top of `assets/release.js`: next version (the top entry is the last), today's date, 2 to 4 changes
   (what the user gets, the limits, how it was checked, which mods it goes with). The home page shows it as is (the
   flasher only its version), so write it for musicians. Then run `python3 tools/gen_changelog.py`: it writes the
   same releases into the repository's `CHANGELOG.md` (never edit that file by hand). Nothing posts it anywhere else:
   Modded-Cycles' Discord workflow went with `.github/` at the fork.
7. Bump the version stamp: the same `YYYY-MM-DD-NN` string in `window.MC_BUILD` (`flasher/index.html`) and every
   `?v=` of the six pages (`index.html`, `flasher/index.html`, `manager/index.html`, `uploader/index.html`,
   `wavetables/index.html`, `guide/index.html`; the Wavetables page by running its generator again, or by hand: its
   `--check` ignores the stamp). A stale stamp
   makes browsers mix a new page with cached old scripts.

**An added machine** with a package (`tools/machines/<id>/manifest.json`, notes/59 to 61) is a machine card:
`"package": "<id>"` in `FEATURES` instead of `variants`, and no `excludes` by hand: `gen_flasher_tweaks.py` computes
them from the package's tweak alone (its generator writes it, with its `conflicts`) against the other cards offered,
and embeds the package and the registrar's frame. The page composes the ticked machine cards into one tweak, where
the first of them is, its id their packages' ids joined by `+`; `ref_mainos.py` composes each set of them with
`tools/compose.py`, one `REF_MODS` entry per set. Steps 2 to 7 above hold as for any card.

The home page list (`ol.mods` in `index.html`, each item with the controls it lights in `data-hl` and the screen
text in `data-lcd`) is a short selection, not every mod: add an item only when the mod is a headline one, and ask.

When the maintainer has tested it on their Model:Cycles: `status` to `"tested"` in `FEATURES` (regenerate
`tweaks.js`), the guide's *Tested* line, the release entry ("Tested on a Model:Cycles"), and the stamp again.
