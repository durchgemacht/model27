3SAMPLE machine (Cycles One-Shot)

By durchgemacht · MIT license · this section is in English only for now

One extra machine, SAMPLE, plays a sample from the +Drive once per trig or in a loop, forward or reverse, the way the Model:Samples does: 6-tap interpolation at 96 kHz, then the Model:Cycles' own decimator and amp chain, and the Model:Samples' filter. The sample is chosen in the PRESET browser and saved with the sound, so projects, kits, presets and sound locks keep it. It is the Cycles One-Shot machine, built here from its own code at version 0.11.

  1. Put samples on the +Drive first: with Elektron Transfer, or with One-Shot's upload page in Chrome or Edge, which works with the official OS too. Samples left there by the Model:Samples OS work as they are.
  2. Press MACHINES and pick SAMPLE, the 7th machine, after Chord. Its icon is Tone's.
  3. Open the PRESET browser (FUNC + MACHINE) on that track and open a sample file: the sound takes the sample's name and plays it. Hold a step while you open a sample and the step gets it as a sound lock.
  4. Shape the sound with the four knobs (table below). DECAY starts at 127 so a sample plays out; GATE is heard with a lower DECAY; PUNCH works as on Tone. PITCH, the keys and FINE set the pitch as on every machine: PITCH 0 at note 60 plays the sample as recorded, whatever its sample rate.
  5. Hold RETRIG and press PUNCH to switch Loop on or off, GATE for Reverse. While RETRIG is held, PUNCH's and GATE's LEDs show the two switches instead of PUNCH and GATE. Loop plays from Start to the end again and again with a hard seam, as on the Model:Samples, so a single-cycle waveform loops without a step; the note then sounds until DECAY closes it (with GATE: until the note ends, then DECAY). Switched off while it plays, it runs to the end and fades out. Reverse plays from the end back to Start, from the next trig on; with both on it loops backwards. Both are saved with the sound.
  6. The main screen shows a narrow column on the left: the current pattern's number, TRAK with the track's number, and the track's level as a bar that moves while you turn LEVEL. To its right the sample's name sits in a box, ending with L and R while Loop and Reverse are on, the waveform below it; two lines mark where a trig starts and stops, and move as you turn COLOR and SHAPE.
KnobName on screenWhat it does
COLORStart (STRT)where the trig starts, 0 to 120 across the sample; moving it keeps the length
SHAPELength (LEN)how far it plays, 0 to 120: the trig ends at Start + Length, at most at the sample's end (120 plays to the end)
SWEEPCutoff (FREQ)the Model:Samples' filter: lowpass to the left (-64 closes it at 5 Hz), off at 0, highpass to the right (63 closes it at 19 kHz)
CONTOURReso (RESO)the filter's resonance, 0 to 127, up to a +27 dB peak at the cutoff
RETRIG + PUNCHLLoop, on or off, at once
RETRIG + GATERReverse, on or off, from the next trig
Sound
one voice per track, once per trig or in a loop. No clicks at the edges: 4 ms fades at the end of what a trig plays, and at its start when Start lies inside the sample; a trig that cuts a sounding note brings the new one in over 0.67 ms, as on the Model:Samples. The filter fades rather than switches across the centre, so a square LFO on Cutoff does not click; at Cutoff 0 the track is untouched, bit for bit. Stereo files play as mono; files at other rates keep their pitch.
Samples
the sample memory is 47 MB of RAM the Model:Cycles' OS leaves unused. Samples load in the background when a project, kit or sound names them, and unload when nothing does.
Limits
Loop and Reverse are switches, not parameters: no p-lock, no LFO. The filter runs before Volume + Dist, where the Model:Samples has it after: with DIST above 0, the distortion acts on the filtered signal.
Other mods
goes with the sequencer, screen, MIDI and USB mods. Not with MACRO, ACID, the Syntakt engines, Model-TG, the per-track filter, sample preview or LFO 2, 3, 4 for now: the other machines take the same place in the firmware and there is no combined build with SAMPLE yet; Model-TG and the per-track filter change the same sound record functions; LFO 2, 3, 4 keeps LFO 2 where SAMPLE saves Loop and Reverse.
Tested
One-Shot 0.7 on a Model:Cycles (2026-10-21): samples open and play, pitch, Start and Length, the filter, several SAMPLE tracks, saved projects, sound locks, the waveform view; One-Shot 0.9 (two boxes above the waveform, 2026-10-24) and 0.10 (the column on the left with the pattern, the track and its level, 2026-10-25), each in its own build in the digiemu emulator and on a Model:Cycles. This page builds One-Shot 0.11 composed from its compiled object by model27's registrar runtime, which differs from that firmware in how the machine is registered (a runtime reading a parameter block instead of detours assembled per build), at three instructions (the machine's index read as a symbol), and by the builder's shortcut layer, which gives SAMPLE its two switches (One-Shot's own firmware plays one shot, forward). Flashed from this page, that build of 0.8 was tested in the digiemu emulator and on a Model:Cycles (2026-10-22); the same runtime also ran there with a second machine next to SAMPLE. Flashed from this page, its build of 0.10 was tested on a Model:Cycles (2026-10-25). Its build of 0.11 (Loop, Reverse, the fade-in after a cut) was tested in the digiemu emulator and, flashed from this page, on a Model:Cycles (2026-10-26).

Before flashing another choice or the official firmware, set your SAMPLE tracks back to an original machine (Kick to Chord): the official OS doesn't have SAMPLE, and a project that still names it can freeze its MACHINES page.

4Digital Delay (effect)Digital Delay (effet)

Based onD'après PPPCCC-Model-Cycles-1.13-Mods by gialloinfuso · MIT licensede gialloinfuso · licence MIT

A second delay for the delay slot: the opposite of the dark stock delay, bright and clean, up to 5.4 seconds, each repeat a little thinner in the lows. Each pattern chooses its delay.Un second delay pour l'emplacement du delay : l'inverse du delay d'origine, sombre ; celui-ci est clair et net, jusqu'à 5,4 secondes, et chaque répétition est un peu plus fine dans les graves. Chaque pattern choisit son delay.

  1. Turn up a track's Delay Send, as for the stock delay.Montez le Delay Send d'une piste, comme pour le delay d'origine.
  2. Hold TRACK and turn Delay Time to the right: after four detents the pattern switches to Digital, and the two delay knobs read Digi Time and Digi Fdbk. Four detents to the left switch it back to the stock Delay.Tenez TRACK et tournez Delay Time vers la droite : au bout de quatre crans, le pattern passe à Digital, et les deux potards du delay s'appellent Digi Time et Digi Fdbk. Quatre crans vers la gauche le ramènent au Delay d'origine.
  3. Delay Time and FUNC + Delay Time (Fdbk) work as usual: Time follows the tempo up to 5.4 seconds, and Fdbk at 127 keeps the repeats going almost endlessly.Delay Time et FUNC + Delay Time (Fdbk) marchent comme d'habitude : Time suit le tempo jusqu'à 5,4 secondes, et Fdbk à 127 garde les répétitions presque sans fin.
  4. Save the project: each pattern keeps its own choice, and changing pattern changes the delay with it.Enregistrez le projet : chaque pattern garde son choix, et un changement de pattern change le delay avec lui.
SoundSon
mono: the two sides of the send summed, the same repeat on both. A high-pass of about 240 Hz and a low-pass of about 12.8 kHz sit in the feedback. It starts from silence each time it is chosen: no old echo comes back.mono : les deux côtés du send additionnés, la même répétition des deux côtés. Un passe-haut d'environ 240 Hz et un passe-bas d'environ 12,8 kHz sont dans la réinjection. Il repart du silence chaque fois qu'il est choisi : aucun ancien écho ne revient.

9RecoverySecours

If the Model:Cycles no longer starts, its startup menu can always reinstall the OS: it lives in an area that updates never write.Si le Model:Cycles ne démarre plus, son menu de démarrage peut toujours réinstaller l'OS : il vit dans une zone que les mises à jour n'écrivent jamais.

  1. With the Model:Cycles off, hold FUNC and turn it on (press and hold MAIN VOLUME).Model:Cycles éteint, maintenez FUNC et allumez-le (appui long sur MAIN VOLUME).
  2. Press TRIG 4 (OS UPGRADE): the screen shows READY TO RECEIVE.Appuyez sur TRIG 4 (OS UPGRADE) : l'écran affiche READY TO RECEIVE.
  3. Send the official OS file through a USB-MIDI interface connected to the MIDI IN.Envoyez le fichier d'OS officiel par une interface USB-MIDI reliée au MIDI IN.

The startup menu doesn't listen to the USB port, only to the MIDI IN (3.5 mm jack, or DIN through the adapter supplied with the Model:Cycles). The full procedure is on GitHub.Le menu de démarrage n'écoute pas le port USB, seulement le MIDI IN (jack 3,5 mm, ou DIN avec l'adaptateur fourni avec le Model:Cycles). La marche à suivre complète est sur GitHub.
