1Before you start

  • A Model:Cycles and a USB cable that carries data (some cables only charge).
  • Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer. Firefox and Safari have no Web MIDI: there the page offers the .syx to download, for Elektron Transfer.
  • The official OS file model-cycles_OS1.13.syx, from elektron.se, unzipped.
  • Your projects backed up with Elektron Transfer.

The Samples OS tab (on this page of the flasher) needs one more file, the official model-samples_OS1.13.syx. The SAMPLE machine needs only the Model:Cycles file.

No firmware is provided here. The page modifies your own file on your computer and checks the result against a reference build before sending it.

2Flashing

  1. In the flasher, pick the cards you want: machines, effects and mods, and they combine. Details on a card gives its full description and a link to its page in this guide. Then press Install under the cards.
  2. Drop your OS file. The firmware is built and checked in the page.
  3. Close Elektron Transfer, connect the Model:Cycles over USB and turn it on normally: no menu to open. Allow MIDI in the browser: the page finds the Model:Cycles and shows its OS version.
  4. Flash: about 30 seconds. Then confirm the update on the Model:Cycles screen with YES. It writes the firmware and restarts by itself: don't turn it off before.

The page uses the update protocol of Elektron Transfer, worked out by Elektroid: the Model:Cycles checks every block as it arrives. You can also download the prepared .syx in step 4 and drop it onto Elektron Transfer's window.

If the page doesn't find the Model:Cycles, download the .syx in step 4 and install it with Elektron Transfer: just as fast.

Keep the flasher tab in the foreground during the transfer: browsers slow down background tabs.

3SAMPLE

By durchgemacht · MIT license

One extra machine, SAMPLE, plays a sample from the +Drive once per trig or in a loop, forward or reverse, the way the Model:Samples does: 6-tap interpolation at 96 kHz, then the Model:Cycles' own decimator and amp chain, and the Model:Samples' filter. The sample is chosen in the PRESET browser and saved with the sound, so projects, kits, presets and sound locks keep it. It is the Cycles One-Shot machine, built here from its own code at version 0.12.

  1. Put samples on the +Drive first: with Elektron Transfer, or with One-Shot's upload page in Chrome or Edge, which works with the official OS too. Samples left there by the Model:Samples OS work as they are.
  2. Press MACHINES and pick SAMPLE, the 7th machine, after Chord. Its icon is Tone's.
  3. Open the PRESET browser (FUNC + MACHINE) on that track and open a sample file: the sound takes the sample's name and plays it. Hold a step while you open a sample and the step gets it as a sound lock.
  4. Shape the sound with the four knobs (table below). DECAY starts at 127 so a sample plays out; GATE is heard with a lower DECAY; PUNCH works as on Tone. PITCH, the keys and FINE set the pitch as on every machine: PITCH 0 at note 60 plays the sample as recorded, whatever its sample rate.
  5. Hold RETRIG and press PUNCH to switch Loop on or off, GATE for Reverse. While RETRIG is held, PUNCH's and GATE's LEDs show the two switches instead of PUNCH and GATE. Loop plays from Start to the end again and again with a hard seam, as on the Model:Samples, so a single-cycle waveform loops without a step; the note then sounds until DECAY closes it (with GATE: until the note ends, then DECAY). Switched off while it plays, it runs to the end and fades out. Reverse plays from the end back to Start, from the next trig on; with both on it loops backwards. Both are saved with the sound.
  6. The main screen shows a narrow column on the left: the current pattern's number, TRAK with the track's number, and the track's level as a bar that moves while you turn LEVEL. To its right the sample's name sits in a box, ending with L and R while Loop and Reverse are on, the waveform below it; two lines mark where a trig starts and stops, and move as you turn COLOR and SHAPE.
  7. Hold a trig and turn COLOR or SHAPE to lock its Start or Length: the whole screen shows the waveform for that trig, at full width, the two lines on its locks (the sound's own Start or Length where it has none), and with a sample lock that sample and its name. With several trigs held, it shows the first with a lock of that knob or a sample lock. The trig's note, velocity and length come back once the value disappears; holding a trig without turning shows them as before.

COLORStart (STRT)where the trig starts, 0 to 120 across the sample; moving it keeps the length
SHAPELength (LEN)how far it plays, 0 to 120: the trig ends at Start + Length, at most at the sample's end (120 plays to the end)
SWEEPCutoff (FREQ)the Model:Samples' filter: lowpass to the left (-64 closes it at 5 Hz), off at 0, highpass to the right (63 closes it at 19 kHz)
CONTOURReso (RESO)the filter's resonance, 0 to 127, up to a +27 dB peak at the cutoff
RETRIG + PUNCHLLoop, on or off, at once
RETRIG + GATERReverse, on or off, from the next trig
Sound
one voice per track, once per trig or in a loop. No clicks at the edges: 4 ms fades at the end of what a trig plays, and at its start when Start lies inside the sample; a trig that cuts a sounding note brings the new one in over 0.67 ms, as on the Model:Samples. The filter fades rather than switches across the centre, so a square LFO on Cutoff does not click; at Cutoff 0 the track is untouched, bit for bit. Stereo files play as mono; files at other rates keep their pitch.
Samples
the sample memory is 47 MB of RAM the Model:Cycles' OS leaves unused. Samples load in the background when a project, kit or sound names them, and unload when nothing does.
Limits
Loop and Reverse are switches, not parameters: no p-lock, no LFO. The filter runs before Volume + Dist, where the Model:Samples has it after: with DIST above 0, the distortion acts on the filtered signal.
Other mods
goes with the sequencer, screen, MIDI and USB mods. Not with MACRO, ACID, the Syntakt engines, Model-TG, the per-track filter, sample preview or LFO 2, 3, 4 for now: the other machines take the same place in the firmware and there is no combined build with SAMPLE yet; Model-TG and the per-track filter change the same sound record functions; LFO 2, 3, 4 keeps LFO 2 where SAMPLE saves Loop and Reverse.
Tested
One-Shot 0.7 on a Model:Cycles (2026-10-21): samples open and play, pitch, Start and Length, the filter, several SAMPLE tracks, saved projects, sound locks, the waveform view; One-Shot 0.9 (two boxes above the waveform, 2026-10-24) and 0.10 (the column on the left with the pattern, the track and its level, 2026-10-25), each in its own build in the digiemu emulator and on a Model:Cycles. This page builds One-Shot 0.12 composed from its compiled object by model27's registrar runtime, which differs from that firmware in how the machine is registered (a runtime reading a parameter block instead of detours assembled per build), at three instructions (the machine's index read as a symbol), and by the builder's shortcut layer, which gives SAMPLE its two switches (One-Shot's own firmware plays one shot, forward). Flashed from this page, that build of 0.8 was tested in the digiemu emulator and on a Model:Cycles (2026-10-22); the same runtime also ran there with a second machine next to SAMPLE. Flashed from this page, its build of 0.10 was tested on a Model:Cycles (2026-10-25). Its build of 0.11 (Loop, Reverse, the fade-in after a cut) was tested in the digiemu emulator and, flashed from this page, on a Model:Cycles (2026-10-26). One-Shot 0.12 (the view of a held trig's locks) was tested in its own build in the digiemu emulator and on a Model:Cycles (2026-10-30); this page's build of 0.12 not yet.

Before flashing another choice or the official firmware, set your SAMPLE tracks back to an original machine (Kick to Chord): the official OS doesn't have SAMPLE, and a project that still names it can freeze its MACHINES page.

4Digital Delay

Based on PPPCCC · MIT license

A second delay for the delay slot: the opposite of the dark stock delay, bright and clean, up to 5.4 seconds, each repeat a little thinner in the lows. Each pattern chooses its delay.

  1. Turn up a track's Delay Send, as for the stock delay.
  2. Hold TRACK and turn Delay Time to the right: after four detents the pattern switches to Digital, and the two delay knobs read Digi Time and Digi Fdbk. Four detents to the left switch it back to the stock Delay.
  3. Delay Time and FUNC + Delay Time (Fdbk) work as usual: Time follows the tempo up to 5.4 seconds, and Fdbk at 127 keeps the repeats going almost endlessly.
  4. Save the project: each pattern keeps its own choice, and changing pattern changes the delay with it.
Sound
mono: the two sides of the send summed, the same repeat on both. A high-pass of about 240 Hz and a low-pass of about 12.8 kHz sit in the feedback. It starts from silence each time it is chosen: no old echo comes back.
Other mods
goes with SAMPLE, POTS and Control All Config. On a firmware without Digital, the official one too, a pattern that chose it plays the stock Delay.
Tested
its sound is PPPCCC's bit for bit, checked in an emulator against their own code. On a Model:Cycles (2026-10-27), from the command line, then flashed from this page alone and with SAMPLE: the choice per pattern, long times and high feedback, switching back and forth, saved projects, six tracks playing.

5POTS

model27's own · MIT license

A delay whose repeats go down an old telephone line: each pass darker and narrower, a little further out of tune, fading in and out and now and then dropping out, with a coarse digital grain on the tail. Each pattern chooses its delay.

  1. Turn up a track's Delay Send, as for the stock delay.
  2. Hold TRACK and turn Delay Time to the right: every four detents the pattern steps to the next delay, the stock Delay, then Digital if it is ticked too, then POTS. With POTS the two delay knobs read POTS Time and POTS Fdbk. To the left it steps back.
  3. Delay Time and FUNC + Delay Time (Fdbk) work as usual: Time follows the tempo as on the stock delay and slides while you turn it, and at the highest Fdbk the repeats go on without running away. The more Fdbk, the further down the line the tail goes: darker, more out of tune, grainier.
  4. Save the project: each pattern keeps its own choice, and changing pattern changes the delay with it.
Sound
mono: the two sides of the send summed, the same repeat on both. The line passes about 350 Hz to 3 kHz, so kicks and low notes come back thin, as down a phone; middle tones come back about as loud as from the stock delay. A hot send (a loud sample, several tracks on the delay) is held at the level the sound was tuned at, so the repeats keep their tone instead of turning to noise. It starts from silence each time it is chosen: no old echo comes back.
Other mods
goes with SAMPLE, Digital Delay and Control All Config. On a firmware without POTS, the official one too, a pattern that chose it plays the stock Delay.
Tested
checked in an emulator on the OS's own code. The build before this one, its line at 24 kHz, was tested on a Model:Cycles: from the command line (2026-10-28), then flashed from this page (2026-10-29). This build runs the line at 12 kHz with an 8-bit codec, as a real phone does, about a fifth lighter on the processor; flashed from this page (2026-10-29), a busy project that lagged with the 24 kHz build plays without lag.

6Trig Condition Order

model27's own · MIT license

The trig conditions in another order on the knob: the A:B conditions come right after the named ones, the percentages last. The conditions themselves stay the same.

  1. Hold a trig and turn CHANCE to the right: FILL, !FILL, PRE, !PRE, NEI, !NEI, 1st, !1st, then 1:2, 2:2, 1:3 … 8:8, then 1% … 99%. To the left it goes back.
  2. The five dots under the value show where you are in that list: dot 1 from FILL to 3:3, dot 2 from 1:4 to 4:6, dot 3 from 5:6 to 3:8, dot 4 from 4:8 to 25%, dot 5 from 33% to 99%.
  3. Fast turns, FUNC + CHANCE and several trigs held at once work as on the stock OS, through the new order.
Patterns
a trig keeps the same condition as on the stock OS: the sequencer plays it and the screen names it as before. Patterns made with or without the mod are the same both ways, the official firmware included.
First turn
a trig without a condition starts at FILL, as on the stock OS.
Other mods
goes with SAMPLE, Digital Delay, POTS and Control All Config.
Tested
checked in an emulator on the OS's own code. Tested on a Model:Cycles from the command line (2026-10-30), the order and the five dots; then flashed from this page and tested on a Model:Cycles (2026-10-30).

7Trig Preview

By drumkilla · MIT license

Hear a single step without starting the sequencer: hold its trig and press PAGE.

  1. Stop the sequencer, or pause it with PLAY.
  2. Hold a trig of the active track and press PAGE: the step sounds as the sequencer would play it, with its note, length, p-locks and sound lock. The page does not turn.
  3. Keep the trig held, change a lock and press PAGE again to hear the step with it.
Always sounds
whatever the trig's condition and probability, also on a muted track. The trig stays.
Limits
while the sequencer plays, PAGE turns the page as on the stock OS.
Other mods
goes with SAMPLE, Digital Delay, POTS, Trig conditions and Control All Config.
Credit
drumkilla's trig preview, version 1.2.0 (MIT), unchanged. Since that version it also plays with the sequencer paused, a fix that came from Modded-Cycles.
Tested
checked in an emulator on the OS's own code: stopped, playing, paused and after a MIDI Stop. Tested on a Model:Cycles (2026-10-04) with the same code inside Model-TG; then flashed from this page and tested on a Model:Cycles (2026-10-30).

8Control All Config

By durchgemacht

Leave single tracks out of Control All: with TRACK held, a knob changes only the tracks you chose.

  1. Hold TRACK and press TRIG 1–6: that track leaves Control All. Press again to put it back.
  2. While TRACK is held, TRIG 1–6 light for the tracks that follow Control All and stay dark for the ones left out.
  3. Hold TRACK and turn a knob: it changes the tracks that follow. The active track always follows, as on the Digitakt and Digitone, so what the screen shows is what the knob does.
Also
left-out tracks keep their machine, LFO destination and PUNCH/GATE when you change those with TRACK held.
Not saved
after power-on every track follows again, as on the stock OS.
Limits
TRACK + LEVEL/DATA (volume, and pan with FUNC) still moves all six tracks. A left-out track that is the active one shows dark but follows while it is active.
Other mods
goes with SAMPLE.
Credit
Control All Config 1.0 by durchgemacht (MIT), unchanged; placed here in four free sprite masks so that it goes with every other mod, as in Modded-Cycles 1.56.
Tested
by its author on a Model:Cycles, in their own build; then flashed from this page and tested on a Model:Cycles, alone and with SAMPLE (2026-10-22).

9Mods on a Model:Samples

A Model:Samples can run the Model:Cycles OS with the same mods as a Model:Cycles. Whatever the machine, the flasher always installs the Model:Cycles OS. With the SAMPLE machine ticked, it can play the samples already on the +Drive (not yet tried on a Model:Samples).

  1. Back up your samples and projects with Elektron Transfer, then close Transfer.
  2. Open the flasher with the Model:Samples choices. In its Mods tab, under Your machine, choose Model:Samples, on its own OS the first time (the machine starts as a Model:Samples), or Model:Samples, already on the Cycles OS to change mods later. Tick the box about your backup.
  3. Tick your mods as usual. Tick SAMPLE to keep playing samples.
  4. In step 2, load both official OS files: model-cycles_OS1.13.syx, then model-samples_OS1.13.syx.
  5. Turn the Model:Samples on normally, choose Fast (USB), flash, then press YES on its screen. It restarts as a Model:Cycles with your mods.

Coming back

The way back to the Model:Cycles OS goes through the startup menu, which only listens to the MIDI IN (see Recovery). Without a MIDI interface, there is no way back.

From the Samples OS, never install a Model:Samples OS file, neither with CONFIG › UPGRADE nor when Elektron Transfer offers an update: it would also replace the startup menu of your Model:Cycles. Back up your projects first: both OSes use the same storage.

The other way: the Cycles OS on a Model:Samples

A Model:Samples can start as a Model:Cycles and play its synth engines. In the flasher, open the Samples OS tab and choose Model:Samples → Cycles OS. The page needs both official OS files, Model:Cycles then Model:Samples, and sends over USB like any update: the Model:Samples on as usual, Elektron Transfer closed, then YES on its screen.

Coming back over USB: same tab, Model:Samples: back to its OS. The page sends the official Model:Samples OS, unchanged. This works because the Model:Cycles OS put on by the page checks updates with the Model:Samples signature. An unmodified Model:Cycles OS would refuse it.

  • Back up your samples and projects with Elektron Transfer first: both OSes use the same storage.
  • Only on a real Model:Samples. A Model:Cycles running the Samples OS answers as a Model:Samples too: it would take this firmware, then refuse Model:Cycles firmwares over USB until its startup menu reinstalls its OS through the MIDI IN.
  • If the machine does not start properly after the update, either way, switch it off and on again. If it still hangs, hold FUNC while switching it on, then press TRIG 2 (EMPTY RESET): it clears the active project, hence the backup.
  • Under the Cycles OS, the machine refuses Model:Cycles firmwares (official or with mods), even when Elektron Transfer offers one: nothing is written. Leave through “back to its OS”. To add mods, use the Mods tab with Model:Samples, already on the Cycles OS (see Mods on a Model:Samples).
  • A Model:Cycles OS installed another way (another tool, or a Model:Cycles build with mods) refuses “back to its OS”: the machine says the update failed and nothing changes. Such an OS accepts the first choice of the tab, Model:Cycles → Samples OS, which brings the Model:Samples OS back.
  • Safety net: the startup menu of the Model:Samples is never touched. If anything went wrong, it takes the official Model:Samples OS through the MIDI IN, as in Recovery.
  • Experimental: checked in the emulator on both OSes' own update checks and on the Model:Samples startup code that loads the new OS, not yet tried on a real Model:Samples.

11Back to stock

Back to the official firmware, under the cards of the flasher, sends your OS file unchanged, over USB, like a normal update. It is also a good rehearsal before a first mod: it checks the cable and the setup without changing anything.

With the SAMPLE machine, first set your SAMPLE tracks back to an original machine (Kick to Chord): the official OS doesn't have SAMPLE, and a project that still names it can freeze its MACHINES page.

12Recovery

If the Model:Cycles no longer starts, its startup menu can always reinstall the OS: it lives in an area that updates never write.

  1. With the Model:Cycles off, hold FUNC and turn it on (press and hold MAIN VOLUME).
  2. Press TRIG 4 (OS UPGRADE): the screen shows READY TO RECEIVE.
  3. Send the official OS file through a USB-MIDI interface connected to the MIDI IN.

The startup menu doesn't listen to the USB port, only to the MIDI IN (3.5 mm jack, or DIN through the adapter supplied with the Model:Cycles). The full procedure is on GitHub.

13Troubleshooting

Nothing happens on the Model:Cycles screen

Step 3 must say that the Model:Cycles was found. Pick the port named “Model:Cycles” and close Elektron Transfer, Overbridge and your music software (on Windows they lock the port), then try again.

The fast method doesn't find the Model:Cycles, or stops

Turn the Model:Cycles on normally (not in CONFIG › UPGRADE), close Elektron Transfer and click Refresh. If it still fails, download the .syx in step 4 and drop it onto Elektron Transfer's window.

The transfer stops, or the Model:Cycles shows an error

Turn it off and on and flash again. If it stops again, download the .syx in step 4 and install it with Elektron Transfer.

No MIDI port in the list

Use a USB cable that carries data, turn the Model:Cycles on, then click Refresh. Some systems only show a new device after the browser is restarted.

MACHINES freezes on a track

The track uses an added machine that this firmware doesn't have. Flash back the previous choice, set the track to an original machine, then flash the one you want.

The update over USB is refused, or the Model:Cycles doesn't start

Go through the startup menu and the MIDI IN: see Recovery.
